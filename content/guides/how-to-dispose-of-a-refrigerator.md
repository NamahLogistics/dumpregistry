# How to dispose of a refrigerator

A refrigerator is not bulky furniture. It contains **refrigerant** that must not be vented, plus foam and metal that cities route through appliance programs. Treating a fridge like a sofa is how pickups fail and Freon rules get violated.

For the shared pattern across fridges, freezers, and ACs, also read [Freon appliance disposal](/guides/freon-appliance-disposal).

## Legal and safety basics

- Only certified technicians should recover refrigerant
- Do not cut compressor lines to “drain” the unit
- Child-safety door rules matter — some cities require doors removed or secured
- Empty food and wipe spills before anyone hauls it

Freezers follow nearly the same playbook — see [freezer](/materials/freezer).

## Disposal paths that usually work

### Utility or energy-efficiency haul-away

Many utilities pay you or haul free when they retire an old fridge. Ask about proof photos, working-condition rules, and whether window ACs qualify too.

### City appliance / Freon collection

Special loads, Freon appointments, or metal-recycling bulk days appear in many metros. Book through the channel on your city dispose page — not a generic bulky furniture form if Freon is split out.

### Retailer haul-away

Buying a replacement sometimes includes haul-away. Confirm stairs, hookups, and whether the old unit must work.

### Transfer station / scrap with Freon paperwork

Some sites accept Freon appliances only with recovery certification. Gate staff will ask. Bring help — fridges are heavy and awkward.

## HHW is usually wrong

Paint-and-battery HHW buildings typically **reject** refrigerators. Start with appliance programs unless your city guide explicitly says HHW.

## Prep checklist

- Unplug and defrost; dry standing water
- Remove food, shelves that will fall, and loose magnets
- Follow door instructions on the city page
- Measure dollies and doorways before DIY moves
- Reserve elevator time in apartments

## What not to do

- Leave a fridge in an alley with the door latchable
- Pay a scrap buyer who vents Freon on-site
- Put a fridge in a construction roll-off without disclosure
- Assume bulk furniture day includes Freon units
