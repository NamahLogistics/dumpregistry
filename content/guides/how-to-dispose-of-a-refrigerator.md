# How to dispose of a refrigerator (Freon rules)

Refrigerators contain refrigerant that cannot be vented. Cities either collect them on special **appliance / bulky** routes, require certified Freon removal and labeling first, or send you to a transfer station or licensed recycler.

## Prep checklist

1. Empty food and defrost if required.
2. Secure or remove doors for child safety where the city requires it.
3. Read Freon rules before curb set-out — never cut lines yourself.

## Related Freon appliances

Window ACs, freezers, and dehumidifiers often follow the same channel. See [refrigerator](/materials/refrigerator), [air conditioner](/materials/air-conditioner), and [freezer](/materials/freezer) hubs, then your [city guide](/cities).
