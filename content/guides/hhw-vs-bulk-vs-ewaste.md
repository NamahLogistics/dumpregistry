# HHW vs bulk vs e-waste — which channel do you need?

Municipal websites often split the same household mess across three programs. Mixing them up is the most common reason a trip fails.

## Bulky / large-item / heavy trash

**For:** mattresses, sofas, carpets, non-Freon appliances (in many cities), yard debris piles.

**Not usually for:** wet paint, pesticides, automotive fluids, loose trash bags, construction debris from contractors.

Start here: [Materials · Bulky](/materials) and your [city hub](/cities).

## Household hazardous waste (HHW)

**For:** oil-based paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, gasoline, antifreeze, many batteries, propane cylinders (rules vary).

**Often not for:** latex paint (many cities say dry it out for trash), Freon appliances, large TVs, tires, business waste.

Find sites: [Centers](/centers) filtered by material, or the HHW section of a city dispose page.

## E-waste / electronics

**For:** TVs, monitors, computers, printers, phones. Some cities fold small electronics into HHW; others ban TVs at HHW and send you to a CyberCycle-style center or special event.

**Watch for:** size limits (e.g. TVs under 27 inches), appointment rules, and data wiping before drop-off.

See [television](/materials/television) and [e-waste mixed](/materials/e-waste-mixed).

## Quick decision order

1. Is it wet chemistry or fuel? → **HHW**
2. Is it a screen or computer? → **E-waste** (confirm if HHW also takes it)
3. Is it Freon (fridge/AC)? → city Freon rule — often **not** plain bulk and **not** HHW
4. Otherwise oversized household goods → **Bulk**

When in doubt, open the item’s city guide on DumpRegistry and use the official source link before you load the car.
