# Medical waste disposal for households (including Los Angeles)

Search Console is already showing queries like “medical waste disposal los angeles.” Most of those searchers are households — not hospitals. Home-generated waste is usually **sharps**, **unused meds**, and sometimes contaminated dressings. It is not the same as a clinic’s red-bag regulated medical waste contract.

## Split the stream correctly

### Sharps (needles, lancets, syringes)

Use a puncture-resistant container and a pharmacy, mail-back, or city sharps program. Full detail: [medical sharps guide](/guides/how-to-dispose-of-medical-sharps) and [needles](/materials/needles).

### Prescription drugs

Use police/pharmacy take-back boxes or DEA events — [prescription drugs](/materials/prescription-drugs). Do not flush except FDA flush-list drugs when no take-back exists.

### Bandages and non-sharp contaminated trash

Many cities allow double-bagged household trash for non-sharp dressings unless blood quantity is unusual — follow local public-health guidance. When in doubt, ask the sanitation number on your city page.

## Los Angeles area

If you are in LA County / City of Los Angeles:

- Start at DumpRegistry’s [Los Angeles city hub](/california/los-angeles) for verified dispose guides we publish
- Use LA Sanitation and county public-health sharps/meds pages linked from those guides when present
- Do not assume a hospital loading dock will take residential sharps

Apartment dwellers: do not put loose needles in chute trash. Building staff get injured the same way city crews do.

## What “medical waste company” ads mean

Companies that advertise medical waste disposal are usually selling **business** pickup. Households rarely need that contract. If you run a clinic, tattoo shop, or lab, you need a licensed hauler — DumpRegistry’s residential guides are not a substitute for that compliance work.

## What not to do

- Loose needles in a grocery bag
- Meds in the recycling bin
- Sharps in a glass jar that can shatter
- Driving to a random landfill gate with red bags and no appointment
