# How to dispose of lithium-ion batteries safely

Lithium-ion batteries (power tools, phones, e-bike packs, loose cells) are a **fire risk** in trash trucks and landfill compactors. Most cities ban them from carts and send residents to HHW, a battery retailer, or a dedicated drop box.

## Safe prep

- Tape terminals on exposed cells when your program asks for it.
- Keep batteries dry and cool in a non-metal container for transport.
- Do not put swollen or damaged packs in a car cabin without ventilation guidance from the site.

## Typical paths

- Permanent HHW facility
- Retail take-back (big-box / battery specialty)
- City or county collection events

## City-specific answer

Start at the [lithium battery material hub](/materials/lithium-battery), open your city, and follow the official source. Pair with [Centers](/centers) when drop-off is the path.

Never put loose lithium cells in blue recycling or the trash cart unless your city explicitly says otherwise — most do not.
