# How to dispose of a mattress in the U.S.

A mattress almost never belongs in the regular trash cart. Most cities treat it as **bulky / large-item** waste with a zone day, an appointment, or a drop-off station.

## Common city paths

1. **Scheduled bulky day** — set it out only in the published window.
2. **Appointment pickup** — book through 311 or the utility portal.
3. **Citizen drop-off** — haul it to a transfer station or collection center (ID often required).

Fees and item caps vary. Some cities wrap mattresses for bed bugs; others ban wrapping.

## What not to do

- Leave it in an alley or vacant lot (illegal dumping).
- Stuff it into a trash cart.
- Assume every city picks mattresses up free every week.
