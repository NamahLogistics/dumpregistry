# How to dispose of e-waste (TVs, computers, phones)

TVs, monitors, computers, printers, tablets, and phones are usually **e-waste**, not garbage. Many cities ban them from landfill carts and offer free curbside e-waste days, transfer-station drop-off, or HHW/electronics events. CRT televisions are the most restricted.

## Prep

- Wipe personal data from phones, tablets, and smart TVs when possible.
- Confirm whether your city allows TVs on bulky day or only at an electronics site.
- Check CRT rules — some resource centers refuse cathode-ray tubes.
