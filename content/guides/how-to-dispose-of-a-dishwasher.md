# How to dispose of a dishwasher

A dishwasher is a built-in appliance, not curb furniture. Most U.S. cities treat it as **appliance / bulky** waste. It does not contain Freon like a refrigerator, but it still will not fit the regular trash cart and often needs water-line disconnect before anyone will haul it.

This query is already showing up in DumpRegistry search impressions — here is the practical path.

## Before you move it

- Shut off the under-sink water supply and power
- Disconnect the drain hose into a pan; expect residual water
- Cap or plug lines so you do not flood the cabinet
- Measure the path through doorways; kickplates and doors catch on trim
- If you are not comfortable with plumbing, hire disconnect — cheaper than water damage

Built-in units are heavier and more awkward than they look. Two people or an appliance dolly is normal.

## Disposal paths that work

### City bulky or appliance collection

Many metros accept dishwashers on bulky / large-item or appliance days. Book the channel your city uses (zone day vs appointment). Ask whether they require:

- Hoses removed or taped
- Empty of dishes and racks that will fall out
- Ground-floor or curb-only set-out (stairs fees for private haulers)

Open [dishwasher](/materials/dishwasher) then your [city guide](/cities).

### Retailer haul-away

Buying a replacement often includes haul-away of the old unit. Confirm before install day — some crews only haul if they also disconnect.

### Scrap metal / transfer station

Dishwashers are mostly metal. Some scrap yards and transfer stations accept them as appliances. Call about Freon confusion: dishwashers are not Freon units, but staff may ask anyway. Bring proof if the site is residency-limited.

### Private appliance hauler

Use this for tight timelines, upstairs units, or when the city window is weeks out. Get a written quote and ask where the load tips.

## What a dishwasher is not

- Not HHW (unless you somehow stored chemicals inside — remove those first)
- Not blue-bin recycling
- Not the same booking form as a [refrigerator](/guides/how-to-dispose-of-a-refrigerator) when Freon is split out — use the appliance/bulky path your city lists for washers and dishwashers

Related: [washing machine](/materials/washer), [clothes dryer](/materials/dryer), [stove / oven](/materials/stove).

## What not to do

- Leave water lines open and walk away
- Stuff a dishwasher into an apartment dumpster without written permission
- Abandon it on the curb outside the bulky window
- Pay a cash hauler who will not name the tip site
