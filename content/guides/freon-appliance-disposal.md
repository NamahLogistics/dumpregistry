# Freon appliances — why your city treats fridges differently

A sofa and a refrigerator are both “big,” but cities almost never treat them the same. Refrigerators, freezers, and many air conditioners contain **refrigerant** that is illegal to vent. That single fact creates a separate channel in nearly every metro we cover.

## The four patterns you will see

1. **Special appliance / Freon route** — You call 311 or the utility, or book a “Special Load,” and crews collect sealed units (examples: Raleigh Special Load, OKC Freon scheduling).
2. **Label / evacuate first** — Curbside bulky is allowed only after Freon is removed and the unit is tagged (Virginia Beach-style rules).
3. **Doors-off recycling week** — The city accepts Freon units on a metal/recycling bulk day with safety rules (Minneapolis doors-removed pattern).
4. **Drop-off only** — No curb Freon; take the unit to a transfer station, MMRF, or cleanup event (Las Vegas transfer fees; Omaha seasonal cleanup sites).

HHW facilities usually **reject** Freon appliances even when they take paint and batteries. Do not start at HHW for a fridge unless your city guide explicitly says otherwise.

## What to do before you move the unit

- Empty food and wipe spills.
- Follow the door rule on your city page (remove, tape, or leave as instructed).
- Keep the sealed system intact unless a certified tech is recovering refrigerant.
- Check whether washers/dryers share the Freon path — often they do **not**.
