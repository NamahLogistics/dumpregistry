# How to dispose of tires

Passenger tires are rarely accepted in ordinary trash carts. Typical paths:

1. **Tire retailer take-back** when you buy replacements
2. **County / city transfer station** (often rimless, capped per visit)
3. **Special tire amnesty events**

## Prep

- Remove rims if the site requires it.
- Count tires — many sites cap 4–5 per household visit.
- Skip curbside bulky unless your city explicitly lists tires.

## Start local

Open the [tires material hub](/materials/tires), pick your city, and follow the official program. Use [Centers](/centers) for verified drop-off sites.

Leaving tires in an alley or vacant lot is illegal dumping in nearly every U.S. city.
