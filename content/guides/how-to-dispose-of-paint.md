# How to dispose of paint (latex vs oil-based)

Paint rules split on chemistry. **Latex / acrylic (water-based)** is often dried solid for trash. **Oil-based / alkyd** almost always goes to household hazardous waste (HHW).

## Latex paint

Many cities tell residents to mix latex with cat litter or hardener until solid, then place the can (lid off once solid) in trash. Some cities still want liquid latex at HHW — check your city page.

## Oil-based paint

Keep the lid on. Take it to an HHW facility or event within local volume limits. Do not pour it down a drain or storm sewer.
